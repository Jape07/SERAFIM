About

SERAFIM plays SPHEREx images in time so a change in the sky can be watched frame by frame. This site shows the three watched fields: the North Ecliptic Pole, the Proserpina crossing, and the Mira variable. The frames are simulated gold, the same tables the real pipeline will publish, until real cutouts replace them.

A candidate is an unidentified source. It is not called a new object.
