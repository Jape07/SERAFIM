Where the frames come from

SERAFIM shows how the SPHEREx infrared sky changes, exposure by exposure. A candidate is an unidentified source. It is not presented as a new object.

The data are from NASA's SPHEREx mission and are served by IRSA at IPAC (Caltech). Background frames shown here are NASA/JPL-Caltech reference images.
